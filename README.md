# 🌌 LOST IN SPACE

> **A journey through the universe, one discovery at a time.**

**LOST IN SPACE** is an immersive 3D web experience designed to make exploring space feel less like browsing a website and more like travelling through the universe.

Instead of presenting astronomy as a collection of static facts, the experience takes users on a visual journey from Earth, through its orbit, across the Solar System, and beyond.

---

## 🌠 About

Space is vast, mysterious, and difficult to experience firsthand.

**LOST IN SPACE** was created to bring a small piece of that experience into the browser.

The website uses an interactive 3D environment to let users explore celestial bodies and discover information about them while moving through a cinematic space journey.

The experience currently takes users through:

**Earth → Orbit → Moon → Mars → Jupiter → Saturn → Uranus → Neptune → Beyond**

---

## ✨ Features

### 🌍 Earth

The journey begins with Earth, presented as an interactive 3D globe surrounded by stars.

Users can visually explore the planet while being introduced to the story behind the experience.

### 🛰️ The Orbit

The experience transitions from Earth into an orbital view, visualizing the relationship between Earth and objects travelling around it.

### 🌙 Celestial Exploration

Users can travel through different parts of the Solar System and encounter major celestial bodies.

Currently featured:

- Moon
- Mars
- Jupiter
- Saturn
- Uranus
- Neptune

### 🪐 Interactive Planet Views

Each featured planet is presented as a large-scale 3D object with its own visual scene and information panel.

Users can discover basic information and facts about each celestial body while exploring.

### 🌌 Cinematic Transitions

Smooth camera movement, zooming, transitions, and visual effects are used to make the experience feel like a continuous journey rather than separate webpages.

### ✨ Beyond

The journey concludes with **BEYOND**, representing the idea that space exploration doesn't end at the edge of our Solar System.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **Three.js** — 3D rendering and scene creation
- **WebGL** — Browser-based 3D graphics
- **JavaScript** — Interactions and application logic
- **HTML5** — Website structure
- **CSS3** — Styling and visual design
- **3D Models & Textures** — Celestial bodies and space environment

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

The visual design of **LOST IN SPACE** focuses on:

- Minimal UI
- Dark space-inspired environments
- Cinematic transitions
- Large-scale 3D objects
- Subtle typography
- Information revealed during exploration

The goal was to keep the interface unobtrusive so that the **3D environment remains the primary focus**.
